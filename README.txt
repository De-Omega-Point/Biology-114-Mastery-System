How to use:
1. Extract the zip.
2. Open index.html in Chrome, Edge, Safari, or Firefox.
3. Notes, tracker, and personal plan save in your browser on that device.
4. Export buttons download .txt files to your device.
5. Print Study Pack opens a print-friendly version.

- syllabus planner
- quiz lab
- flashcards
- notes vault
- export tools
- mastery tracker
